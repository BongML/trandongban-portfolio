Workflows I've deployed

Real automations, from the canvas

Six examples, shown as they were built. Click a diagram to open it at full size.

01 · Make.com

Auto-publishing from Instagram, Facebook and YouTube Shorts

A Google Sheet of source links drives the whole run. A router sends each link down its platform's branch, where the real video ID is resolved, the file is downloaded and published as a Facebook Reel, and the row is updated with the result.

02 · Make.com · AI

TikTok video translation and dubbing

Automatically translates and dubs TikTok videos into a specified target language, generates a fitting caption with AI, and saves the results to Google Sheets.

03 · n8n · Meta Graph API

Bulk fanpage setup: avatar, cover, bio and website

Loops through pages listed in a sheet, gets each page's access token, then uploads and sets the avatar and cover, and adds the bio and website link when provided. Every failed step is logged back to the sheet, with a 30–60 second pause between pages.

04 · n8n · AI agent

Automatic candidate replies on a recruitment fanpage

Incoming messages arrive by webhook. Images are analysed first, then an AI agent with conversation memory answers from recruitment documents and an FAQ kept in Google Sheets, and sends its reply back to the candidate.

05 · n8n · Meta Graph API

Automated monthly reports with data pulled from Meta

On the 1st of each month, the workflow goes through every unreported page in a sheet, gets its access token, pulls last month's numbers from Meta and writes them back. Reels above a view threshold are added to a Top Reels tab, and a second trigger on the 28th clears the sheet for the next cycle.

06 · Tool · AI video

Verda: video ads from an Amazon product link

A tool I deployed that generates a video ad from an Amazon product link, with an AI script, voiceover and avatar.
